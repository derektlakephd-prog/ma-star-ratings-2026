# Glossary

**Analytic cohort.** The 425 contracts included in the rating and payment analyses: MA-PD contracts with both a CMS-published and a simulated overall rating, not disaster affected, rated after measure removal, and listed in the May 2026 enrollment file.

**Attainment scale.** A 0 to 100 scale on which 100 is the maximum attainable value of a measure and higher values indicate better performance. Percentages where higher is better are used directly; percentages where lower is better (readmissions, members choosing to leave the plan) become 100 minus the rate; complaint rates per 1000 enrollees become the percentage of members without a complaint, for display only.

**Baseline rating.** A contract's 2026 rating simulated with all measures and the published reward factor thresholds. Baseline QBP eligibility uses this rating, so that baseline and projected eligibility come from the same algorithm.

**Benchmark.** The monthly per-enrollee amount against which MA plans bid, set by county in the CMS ratebook. Contracts rated 4 stars or higher receive a benchmark that includes a quality bonus of 5 percentage points, or 10 in qualifying (double bonus) counties; the ratebook's 5% bonus rate reflects both.

**Bottom decile.** The 10th percentile of the contract distribution of a measure. A measure is topped out when its bottom-decile contract reaches at least 90 on the attainment scale.

**Categorical adjustment index (CAI).** A factor CMS adds to each summary and overall rating to adjust for the within-contract disparity in performance associated with the share of enrollees who are dual eligible, low income subsidy recipients, or disabled. Contracts are assigned to a final adjustment category, and each category has a published value. This analysis holds the CAI at its published 2026 value.

**CMS.** Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services.

**Contract.** The agreement between CMS and an MA organization or prescription drug plan sponsor. Star Ratings are assigned at the contract level. A contract can include many plans.

**Contract type.** The rating type used in this analysis. MA-PD contracts receive Part C, Part D, and overall ratings; MA-only contracts receive a Part C rating; PDP contracts receive a Part D rating.

**CPSC.** The CMS Monthly Enrollment by Contract/Plan/State/County files, which report enrollment by plan and county with each plan's type, SNP status, and parent organization.

**Cut points.** The thresholds CMS sets for each measure to convert raw performance into 1 to 5 measure stars. Measure stars are unchanged by measure removal and are taken as published.

**Disaster policy.** The CMS extreme and uncontrollable circumstances policy. Contracts with a minimum share of enrollees in FEMA-designated disaster areas receive the higher of their current-year and prior-year measure stars on affected measures. The measure-level inputs are not public, so the 64 disaster-affected contracts (at least 25% of enrollees affected in 2023 or 2024) are excluded. All 64 qualify on 2024 enrollment.

**Enrollment.** May 2026 contract enrollment from the State/County/Contract file. CMS suppresses county counts of 10 or fewer; these are counted as 0.

**FEMA.** Federal Emergency Management Agency.

**HEDIS.** Healthcare Effectiveness Data and Information Set, the source of several clinical measures.

**HMO.** Health maintenance organization. In this analysis, CPSC plan type HMO/HMOPOS.

**Hold harmless (improvement measures).** CMS computes the highest-level rating of each contract with and without the improvement measures. If the rating without them is at least 4 stars and higher than the rating with them, it replaces the rating with them.

**Improvement measures.** C30 (Health Plan Quality Improvement) and D04 (Drug Plan Quality Improvement), which score change in a contract's performance over time. They have no raw rate and are not classified.

**MA.** Medicare Advantage, the private plan alternative to traditional Medicare (Part C).

**MA-PD.** An MA contract that also provides Part D prescription drug coverage.

**Measure component.** A single measure as it enters the rating algorithm. Complaints About the Health/Drug Plan and Members Choosing to Leave the Plan each have a Part C and a Part D component, so the 11 removed measures are 13 components.

**Minimum measure threshold.** A contract needs stars on at least half of the applicable non-improvement measures to receive a summary or overall rating. Six contracts fall below it after measure removal.

**MSA.** Medicare medical savings account plan.

**Never.** QBP eligibility group: rated below 4.0 at baseline and after removal.

**New measures.** Measures in their first year of Star Ratings use (in 2026: C04, C05, and C13), assigned a weight of 1. CMS compares ratings with and without new measures only for disaster-affected contracts.

**Overall rating.** The single summary of Part C and Part D performance for an MA-PD contract, computed from the Part C measures and the nonduplicated Part D measures.

**Parent organization.** The corporate owner of a contract, from the CPSC contract information file. Ownership (for-profit or nonprofit) is classified from it.

**Part C.** Medicare Advantage medical coverage. Part C measures are coded C01 to C33.

**Part D.** Medicare prescription drug coverage. Part D measures are coded D01 to D12.

**PDP.** Stand-alone prescription drug plan.

**PFFS.** Private fee-for-service plan.

**Plan.** A benefit package offered under a contract. A contract can offer HMO, PPO, and SNP plans.

**PPO.** Preferred provider organization; in this analysis, CPSC plan types Local PPO and Regional PPO.

**Projected rating.** A contract's 2026 rating simulated without the 13 removed components, with reward factor thresholds recalibrated to the post-removal distribution.

**QBP.** Quality bonus payment: the increase in the MA benchmark for contracts rated 4 stars or higher (5 percentage points, or 10 in qualifying counties). In this analysis, the annual QBP value of a contract is the difference between the 5% bonus and 0% bonus Parts A and B benchmark rates, multiplied by county enrollment and by 12 months.

**QBP eligibility change.** Lose (4.0 or higher at baseline, below 4.0 after removal), Gain (below 4.0, then 4.0 or higher), Retain (4.0 or higher both times), or Never (below 4.0 both times).

**Ratebook.** The CMS file of county MA capitation rates for a payment year, published with the annual Rate Announcement.

**Rate-based measure.** The 2 complaint measures (C28, D02), reported as complaints per 1000 enrollees. Because complaints are rare, every contract sits near the ceiling of the attainment scale, so they are shown in the Figure but not classified.

**Removed measures.** The 11 measures (13 components) removed from the Star Ratings by the CMS final rule published April 6, 2026: C07, C19, C24, C25, C28, C29, C31, C32, C33, D01, D02, D03, D07.

**Replication accuracy.** Agreement between the simulated baseline overall rating and the CMS-published overall rating among the 436 non-disaster contracts with both ratings: 431 match; 5 differ by 0.5 stars.

**Retain.** QBP eligibility group: rated 4.0 or higher at baseline and after removal.

**Reward factor.** An addition of 0.1 to 0.4 stars to a summary or overall rating for contracts whose weighted mean of measure stars is high (at or above the 65th or 85th percentile) and whose variance across measures is low (below the 30th or 70th percentile). The percentiles are computed across contracts.

**Reward factor recalibration.** Recomputing the reward factor thresholds as the same percentiles of the post-removal distribution of contract means and variances.

**Rounding (half-star).** Summary and overall ratings are rounded to the nearest half star, with ties rounded up.

**SNP.** Special needs plan, a plan restricted to dual eligible enrollees, enrollees in institutions, or enrollees with specified chronic conditions.

**Star Ratings.** The CMS 1 to 5 star quality rating system for MA and Part D contracts.

**Summary rating.** A Part C or Part D rating, computed from the measures in that part.

**Topped out.** A measure whose bottom-decile contract reaches at least 90 on the attainment scale, so that even low-performing contracts are within 10 points of the ceiling.

**Weight.** The multiplier CMS assigns to each measure in the weighted mean of measure stars: 1, 2, or 3 for most measures and 5 for the improvement measures.
